Research / Evidence

Inspect the claim behind the result

A recovered edge, a modeled operation, a passing comparison, and a safe rewrite are different claims. HydIR records their evidence separately.

Reading artifact status

ClaimRead it as
Structural recoveryRecovered functions and edges, with unresolved targets retained.
Semantic fidelityExact, conservative, or unknown relative to the stated machine model.
VerificationThe recorded check or finite execution comparison; not_run means no comparison.
Rewrite readinessA separate gate with byte, boundary, and semantic obligations.

Reproducible comparisons

  • Independent Ghidra execution oracleSeeded instruction paths and state compared with Rust; explicit call and user-operation stops.

Some Ghidra comparisons start at a branch with explicit flag seeds because preceding operations remain opaque. Such a result covers the seeded suffix, not whole-function execution from entry. Frida observes paths for one input; a trace alone does not establish exit behavior or equivalence.
